## ⚠️ Read this before using the list

**Trout are the indicator species. The aquifer is the objective.** *(`00_`)*

The Blue Ribbon list is useful because the qualifying criteria are, read sideways, a
description of groundwater-dominated water:

> - Supports **excellent stocks of wild resident trout** *(self-sustaining — not stocked)*
> - Physical characteristics permitting fly casting; **shallow enough to wade**
> - **Diverse insect life and good fly hatches**
> - **Excellent water quality**

**Wild, self-sustaining trout in Michigan means summer temperatures that don't break
~68 °F, and that means continuous groundwater discharge.** That is the whole reason to
start here.

### ⚠️ Three limits on the list, and they matter

1. **⚠️ The DNR no longer officially maintains the designation.** 62 streams earned it;
   it is a **historical quality marker, not a current regulatory status.** The
   *regulatory* layer is **Designated Trout Stream** (`MiEnviro` layer 32), which is a
   different and larger set.
2. **⚠️ It selects for FISHABILITY, not just hydrogeology.** "Permits fly casting,"
   "shallow enough to wade," "good hatches" are angler criteria. **A cold, spring-fed,
   brushy, unwadeable creek — perfect for our purposes — is invisible to this list.**
   Groundwater discharge is far more widespread than 62 streams.
3. **⚠️ It selects for FAME, which is the opposite of what we want on price.** The
   Au Sable, the Pere Marquette and the Manistee have established fishing economies and
   priced frontage. **N 20th Ave's Middle Branch is on the list precisely because it is
   "small, remote" — 6 miles, low name recognition, real quality.** ⭐ **The interesting
   part of this list is its tail, not its head.**

**⭐ So: use it as a seed and as a validation set, and expect the real screen —
`MiEnviro` layer 1, Cold/Cold Transitional Streams — to be materially broader. If the
funnel only ever returns parcels on these 62, the screen is too narrow.**

## ⭐ Where the interesting overlap already is

Counties that appear **both** on this list **and** in prior project work — start here,
because the tax-sale archives, millage sheets, zoning status and county HPI series are
already pulled or half-pulled:

| County | Streams | Prior work |
|---|---|---|
| **Osceola** | ⭐ **Middle Branch**, Pine | ⭐⭐ Deep. Zoned/unzoned township list verified · 42 tax-sale records · FHFA 28%/−2.22% · N 20th Ave file |
| **Missaukee** | Clam | Adjacent research; Lake Missaukee, ORV trails |
| **Roscommon** | Au Sable (main + south) | Deep — Higgins/Houghton region file, FHFA 30%/−2.89% |
| **Crawford / Oscoda / Otsego** | Au Sable, Manistee, Big Creek, Pigeon, Black | Not researched. ⭐ **The densest Blue Ribbon geography in the state** |
| **Montmorency** | Black E. Br., Canada Ck, Gilchrist, Hunt Ck | Thunder Bay River file — Briley Twp's "Economy Efficiency Dwelling" finding lives here |
| **Manistee / Lake / Mason** | Pine, Bear Ck, PM, Little Manistee, Big Sable | Region file started (provisional) |
| **Gladwin / Clare** | Cedar River | Secord Lake file; ⚠️ FetchGIS is weak |
| **Grand Traverse / Kalkaska** | Boardman, Manistee | ⚠️ Best HPI in the state (5%/−0.28%) and the worst prices |

**⭐ The unresearched cluster worth a first pass: Crawford / Oscoda / Otsego / Montmorency.**
Four contiguous counties, the highest concentration of Blue Ribbon miles in Michigan,
extensive state forest, and **no county-level work done yet in either project.**
